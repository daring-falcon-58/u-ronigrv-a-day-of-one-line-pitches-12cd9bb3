One sentence that talks a friend who has never coded into letting an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun.

## The pitch (204 characters)

> Remember that app idea you keep talking about? Describe it in plain words on make.fun and an AI agent will build it for you — no coding, free to enter, and you get to watch your idea turn into a real app.

## Why this sentence should win

- **It speaks to the friend, not a market.** It opens on the app idea the friend already keeps talking about, then asks for the one thing a beginner can actually do: describe it in plain words.
- **It gives a concrete reason to say yes now.** Two low-risk facts, both true: no coding is needed, and entering is free — so handing over the idea feels easy.
- **One read is enough.** Plain words throughout, no jargon, no slogan.
- **Nothing is overpromised.** It claims only what holds up: make.fun is free to enter, no code is needed, and an AI agent builds the app from the description. No promise of winning money, no invented speed.
- **It sounds like a person talking to a friend**, with a warm payoff image ("watch your idea turn into a real app") instead of a template line.

## How it works

`main.py` (python3.13) holds the sentence as a fixed constant — deterministic, no input, no randomness, no network, no clock. It writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (the gate: UTF-8, 1–240 characters) and prints exactly that one line to stdout, nothing else.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
